Temporal line charts: regular spacing\n\nFor temporal line charts, avoid data with very irregular time gaps (e.g., daily data with multi-month gaps). Vega-Lite draws straight lines between points, making large gaps misleading.\n\n**Fix options**:\n- If the user explicitly asks for a line chart with irregular data, use `\"point\": true` to show where real observations exist: `\"mark\": {\"type\": \"line\", \"point\": true}`\n- Otherwise, prefer a Vega-Lite `filter`/`aggregate` transform to produce data with regular time intervals (e.g., fill gaps with zeros or nulls, or aggregate to a coarser granularity like monthly/quarterly).
